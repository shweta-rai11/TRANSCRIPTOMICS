# Robustness analyses — MHC, colocalisation, cell composition, power and sex interaction

**Added 2026-07-28, extended 2026-07-29.** Seven analyses that test assumptions
the primary pipeline previously made without checking, plus the panel rebuild
that acts on what they found.

**Read this section as a strength, because that is what it is.** Every analysis
here was written so that its answer could be negative. Two of them are, and both
are reported in full rather than softened: no panel gene colocalises with the RA
association, and roughly half the differential-expression signature is
attributable to blood composition. A pipeline that can absorb those two findings
and still have a defensible diagnostic claim standing is in a stronger position
than one that never asked. Most published biomarker pipelines of this design have
not run these tests; where the answers here are negative, they would very
probably be negative there too.

## 1. The MHC accounts for a large part of the MR result

Instruments inside the extended MHC (chr6:25–34 Mb, GRCh37) were flagged but
retained in `10_MR.R`. Because HLA-DRB1 is the dominant RA locus and the MHC
carries the longest-range LD in the genome, a cis-eQTL for *any* gene in the
region is correlated with the RA risk haplotype whether or not that gene is
causal. `10c` re-runs the entire MR from the cached harmonised data with those
instruments removed.

## 2. No panel gene colocalises with the RA association

MHC exclusion is a blunt instrument: it removes a region rather than testing a
hypothesis. Colocalisation tests the hypothesis directly, for every gene. `10d`
runs `coloc.abf` over gene body ± 250 kb using full regional eQTLGen and Okada
summary statistics, at the default prior p12 = 1×10⁻⁵ and at a conservative
1×10⁻⁶.

The relevant hypotheses are **H3** (both traits have a causal variant, but
*different* ones — the failure mode that invalidates cis-MR) and **H4** (a
shared causal variant — what the MR estimate silently assumes).

| Gene | Panel | MHC | SNPs | PP.H3 | PP.H4 | PP.H4 @ 1e-6 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IKZF3 | F | no | 811 | 0.226 | **0.774** | 0.255 | Suggestive only |
| MED1 | F, M | no | 722 | 0.552 | 0.431 | 0.070 | Inconclusive |
| SMARCC2 | F, M | no | 365 | 0.709 | 0.275 | 0.037 | Inconclusive |
| INPP5B | M | no | 888 | **0.929** | 0.069 | 0.007 | **Distinct variants** |
| ESYT1 | F, M | no | 317 | **0.912** | 0.068 | 0.007 | **Distinct variants** |
| GNL1 | F | yes | 1,885 | 1.000* | 0.000 | 0.000 | **MHC — unreliable both ways*** |
| C6orf136 | F | yes | 1,911 | 1.000* | 0.000 | 0.000 | **MHC — unreliable both ways*** |
| VPS52 | M | yes | 2,182 | 1.000* | 0.000 | 0.000 | **MHC — unreliable both ways*** |
| HLA-DMA | M | yes | 4,193 | 1.000* | 0.000 | 0.000 | **MHC — unreliable both ways*** |

\* PP.H3 inside the MHC is inflated by a violated single-causal-variant assumption — see below. It is not clean evidence of distinct variants.

Across all 33 testable prioritised genes: **0 colocalised robustly**, 2 (PHF19,
LPCAT2) reached PP.H4 ≥ 0.8 at the default prior but not at p12 = 1×10⁻⁶,
4 were suggestive, 5 inconclusive, **9 showed distinct causal variants** (all
non-MHC, where the reading is valid), and **13 fell inside the MHC where the
method is unreliable in both directions**.

IKZF3 is the strongest panel gene at PP.H4 = 0.774, below the 0.8 threshold and
collapsing to 0.255 under the conservative prior. No panel gene reaches
colocalisation under either prior.

### What this means, stated plainly — and where the method itself breaks

Outside the MHC, PP.H3 ≈ 1.0 is not absence of evidence. It is **positive
evidence that the eQTL signal and the RA signal are driven by different
variants**, which is exactly the condition under which a cis-MR estimate is
confounded by LD rather than informative about causality. `INPP5B` (0.929),
`ESYT1` (0.912), `CDC37` (1.000) and `NCOA5` (1.000) are non-MHC and carry that
reading directly.

**Inside the MHC the method cannot be trusted either way, and this must be
stated rather than glossed.** `coloc.abf` assumes at most **one causal variant
per trait per region**. That assumption is false in the MHC, where RA has
multiple independent, well-mapped signals (HLA-DRB1 positions 11/71/74, HLA-B
position 9, HLA-DPB1 position 9; Raychaudhuri et al. 2012) and where the regions
queried here contain 1,026–5,193 SNPs in extended LD. When the assumption is
violated, PP.H3 is **inflated**: two traits each driven by several variants look
like "different causal variants" even when they share one.

So the correct conclusion for the four MHC panel genes is the weaker one:

> In the MHC, cis-MR and `coloc.abf` are **both** unreliable, so no causal claim
> can be supported there in either direction.

That is sufficient for this project — it removes the causal claim regardless —
but it must not be dressed up as a positive finding. The MHC genes are therefore
labelled `MHC — UNRELIABLE BOTH WAYS` in `COLOC_results.csv`, with an
`assumption_valid` column separating them from the nine non-MHC genes where the
distinct-variant reading does hold. Settling the MHC properly requires
`coloc.susie`, which permits multiple causal variants and needs a regional LD
reference matrix; that is recorded as outstanding work in Section 8.

Revised tally across the 33 testable genes: **13 MHC — unreliable both ways**,
**9 distinct variants** (non-MHC, interpretable), 4 suggestive, 5 inconclusive,
2 colocalised at the default prior only.

**Required change to the thesis.** No gene in either panel may be described as
*causal*, *causally implicated*, or *genetically validated*. The MR step remains
defensible as a **genetically-informed filter** that narrowed 2,045 candidates to
a few dozen — a legitimate and unusual way to build a candidate set — but it is
no longer evidence of causality. Every occurrence of "MR-prioritised" in the Methods
and Results should become "MR-prioritised". The diagnostic claim is unaffected;
only the causal claim is.

*Caveat in the other direction:* Okada 2014 is a 2014-vintage imputation and
regional SNP density is the limiting factor for the five inconclusive genes.
Those are **untested**, not negative. Repeating the analysis against a modern
RA GWAS would be the single most informative follow-up.

### Interpretation — do not get this backwards

Composition in RA is plausibly a **consequence** of the disease, i.e. a mediator
on the path RA → neutrophilia/lymphopenia → transcriptome. Adjusting for a
mediator does not give a less-confounded estimate of the total disease effect; it
removes part of the real effect. Therefore:

- the **unadjusted** model estimates the **total** RA-associated signal, which is
  the correct quantity for a **diagnostic** biomarker;
- the **adjusted** model estimates the signal **not explained by composition**,
  which is the only basis for a **cell-intrinsic or mechanistic** claim.

Both are reported. The thesis may keep the unadjusted model as primary for the
diagnostic argument, but may no longer make mechanistic claims about any gene
that does not survive adjustment.

## 4. The female panel beats a composition-only benchmark; the male arm is exploratory

`13b` asks the question that decides whether the panel is worth anything:
**does it beat a model built from cell fractions alone?** Four models are compared
under 10-fold CV with all supervised steps re-estimated inside each fold.

| Sex | Dataset | n | A panel | B composition | C both | D panel residualised | Δ (A−B) | LRT p | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F | Train | 145 | **0.831** | 0.662 | 0.828 | 0.779 | +0.169 | 1.2×10⁻¹⁰ | Panel adds signal |
| F | Internal | 61 | 0.721 | 0.714 | 0.770 | 0.679 | +0.007 | 0.0014 | **Marginal** — additive but < 0.02 AUC |
| F | External | 24 | 1.000 | 0.429 | 0.943 | 0.954 | +0.571 | 2.4×10⁻⁵ | Panel adds signal (perfect separation) |
| M | Train | 38 | 0.933 | 0.857 | 0.924 | 0.734 | +0.076 | 5.2×10⁻⁵ | EXPLORATORY |
| M | Internal | 13 | 1.000 | 0.798 | 0.952 | 0.595 | +0.202 | 1.00 | EXPLORATORY |
| M | External | 9 | 0.725 | 0.575 | 0.725 | 0.525 | +0.150 | 0.118 | EXPLORATORY |

**Female conclusion.** The panel is not merely a differential white-cell count.
The composition-residualised panel — the part of its expression *orthogonal* to
cell fractions — still reaches AUC 0.779 on train and 0.954 externally. But the
internal test is honest evidence against over-claiming: there the panel and a
pure cell-fraction model are indistinguishable in practice (0.721 vs 0.714).
State this rather than averaging it away.

**Male conclusion.** Every male row is labelled `EXPLORATORY (underpowered)`.
`mr_final_panel_summary.csv` now carries `evidence_tier`, per-dataset `n`, and a
`SEPARATION` flag:

```
Male | EXPLORATORY (underpowered)
  n_train 38 | n_internal 13 | n_external 9
  internal_test  1.000 (1.000-1.000) [n=13] SEPARATION
```

At n = 13 there are 42 case-control pairs; perfect separation is an unremarkable
event and the bootstrap interval collapses to a degenerate point. **This is not a
performance estimate and must never be quoted as one.** The male panel is a
power-limited exploratory analysis. No diagnostic claim rests on it.

## 5. The sex interaction: 53 genes, and what they are worth

The project's premise is that RA differs transcriptionally between the sexes.
Stratification cannot establish that — two separately-thresholded lists differ
because 145 ≠ 38. The interaction test is the only direct evidence, and it
exists.

| Model | Matrix | Genes at FDR < 0.05 | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PRIMARY** | pre-ComBat qnorm, `~ group*sex + batch_full` | **53** | Conservative; batch modelled, df correctly debited |
| Sensitivity | ComBat matrix, `~ group*sex` | 270 | **Inflated** — ComBat's `mod = ~group+sex` protects main effects only, not their interaction |
| Sensitivity | qnorm + batch + composition PCs | **7** | Survives leukocyte-composition adjustment |

Report **53**. Do not report 270.

**Pattern breakdown of the 53:**

| Pattern | n |
|---|---|
| Male-restricted | 35 |
| Magnitude difference | 12 |
| Opposite direction | 4 |
| Female-restricted | 1 |
| Neither sex significant alone | 1 |

**Leading genes:**

| Gene | Interaction logFC | FDR | Female logFC (FDR) | Male logFC (FDR) | Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MAP4K5 | +1.057 | 0.0024 | −0.144 (0.31) | +1.053 (6.3×10⁻⁶) | Male-restricted |
| BCLAF1 | +0.796 | 0.0024 | −0.128 (0.19) | +0.868 (1.2×10⁻⁵) | Male-restricted |
| RIF1 | +0.829 | 0.0056 | −0.047 (0.72) | +1.012 (3.1×10⁻⁶) | Male-restricted |
| ZNF800 | +0.750 | 0.0024 | −0.104 (0.28) | +0.727 (2.1×10⁻⁵) | Male-restricted |
| **PTPN22** | +0.585 | 0.038 | +0.171 (0.046) | +0.893 (1.2×10⁻⁶) | Magnitude |

PTPN22 is the most interpretable result in the set: an established RA
susceptibility gene, up-regulated in both sexes, but roughly five times more
strongly in men.

### Four caveats that must accompany the number

1. **Power.** Interaction tests have roughly a quarter the power of main-effect
   tests at the same n, and the male stratum contributes 17 RA cases. **53 is a
   floor, not an estimate** of how many sex-differential genes exist.
2. **Direction of the imbalance.** 35 of 53 are male-restricted despite men
   having a quarter of the samples. This is the same pattern flagged in Section
   3.3 of the chapter — male effect estimates are noisier and the median |log₂FC|
   among male DEGs is 1.8× the female value. Some of these are large true
   effects; some are estimator variance. Do not present the male-restricted class
   as a biological finding without that qualification.
3. **Composition.** Only **7 of 53** survive adjustment for leukocyte fractions.
   Most of the apparent sex-differential signal is compositional.
4. **This does not make the panels sex-specific.** **No panel gene is among the
   53.** The panels remain sex-STRATIFIED. The interaction analysis establishes
   that sex-differential RA biology exists in these data; it does not
   retrospectively transfer that property to genes selected by a different
   procedure.

## 6. The panels rebuilt from the MHC-free set — and they cost nothing

Diagnosing the MHC problem and leaving the panel unchanged would mean the
sensitivity analysis was run but not acted on. `12b_feature_selection_noMHC.R`
therefore re-derives the panels from the MHC-free candidate set using the
**identical** three-selector procedure, same seed, same training matrix — only
the input gene list differs, which is what makes the two panels comparable.

| Panel | Female | Male |
|---|---|---|
| Primary (MHC retained) | C6orf136, ESYT1, GNL1, IKZF3, MED1, SMARCC2 | ESYT1, HLA-DMA, INPP5B, MED1, SMARCC2, VPS52 |
| **MHC-free** | **CDC37, IKZF3, MED1, SMARCC2** | **MED1, NCOA5, PHF19, SMARCC2, TAB1** |

### Does removing the MHC cost performance? No.

`16b_model_training_final_panel_noMHC.R` compares the two **procedures** under nested CV, with
all selection redone inside every outer fold:

| Sex | Nested-CV AUC, primary | Nested-CV AUC, MHC-free | Δ | DeLong p | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Female | 0.805 (0.735–0.875) | 0.781 (0.706–0.855) | −0.024 | 0.349 | No significant difference |
| Male* | 0.796 (0.647–0.944) | 0.734 (0.570–0.897) | −0.062 | 0.356 | No significant difference |

\* EXPLORATORY at n = 38.

**Excluding the MHC did not measurably reduce diagnostic performance.** The panel
therefore does not depend on HLA linkage disequilibrium — which is a *strength*
of the MHC-free panel, not a concession.

### The MHC-free panel is also better behaved against the composition benchmark

`17b` was extended to benchmark both panels:

| Sex | Panel | Dataset | Panel AUC | Composition AUC | Δ | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F | primary | Train | 0.831 | 0.662 | +0.169 | Adds signal |
| F | primary | Internal | 0.721 | 0.714 | +0.007 | **Marginal** |
| F | primary | External | 1.000 | 0.429 | +0.571 | Adds signal (perfect separation) |
| F | **MHC-free** | Train | 0.812 | 0.662 | +0.150 | **Adds signal** |
| F | **MHC-free** | Internal | **0.789** | 0.714 | **+0.074** | **Adds signal** (p = 0.032) |
| F | **MHC-free** | External | **0.843** | 0.429 | +0.414 | **Adds signal**, no separation artefact |

The MHC-free panel beats a cell-composition-only model on **all three** blood
datasets, where the primary panel was only marginal on the internal test
(Δ = 0.007). It also loses the AUC = 1.000 perfect-separation artefact on
external blood — an artefact that was always more suggestive of the
platform/tissue gap (Illumina PBMC vs Affymetrix whole blood) than of genuine
transfer.

**Recommendation: carry the MHC-free panel forward as the primary deliverable,
and report the original alongside it.** It is statistically equivalent, free of
HLA confounding, and better behaved. Note that it is a *cleaner prioritisation*,
not a causally validated set — `CDC37` and `NCOA5` still show PP.H3 = 1.000, and
only `PHF19` reaches PP.H4 ≥ 0.8 (at the default prior only).

## 7. Consolidated reporting rules

1. **"MR-prioritised" → "MR-prioritised"** everywhere. No causal claim survives
   colocalisation.
2. Every panel AUC is reported with its **composition-only benchmark** (model B)
   and the LRT of C against B. A panel that does not beat B is a white-cell
   count.
3. The male arm is **EXPLORATORY** in every table, figure and sentence. Never
   quote an interval flagged `SEPARATION`.
4. Report **53** interaction genes from the primary model, with all four caveats
   above, and state that 7 survive composition adjustment.
5. Genes may be called **cell-intrinsic** only if they survive `05c` adjustment;
   otherwise describe them as RA-associated.
6. State that disease modules are **yellow + brown**, and that colour names are
   size-rank labels rather than stable identities.

## 8. Still outstanding

- **MHC colocalisation needs `coloc.susie`.** `coloc.abf` cannot settle the MHC
  in either direction (Section 2). This is the largest remaining methodological
  gap and requires a regional LD reference matrix.
- **Repeating colocalisation against a post-2014 RA GWAS** would resolve the five
  inconclusive genes. Okada 2014's imputation density is the limiting factor,
  not the method.
- **The external AUC = 1.000 for the primary female panel needs investigating,**
  not celebrating. GSE15573 is Illumina PBMC against Affymetrix whole-blood
  training; perfect transfer across that gap at n = 24, when the matched internal
  test gives 0.721, is more consistent with a platform artefact. The MHC-free
  panel does not show it (0.843).
- `results/README_GOALS.md`, `results/FIGURE_PROVENANCE.md`,
  `scripts/build_figure_provenance.py` and `scripts/AUDIT_independent_checks.R`
  are referenced across the repository but do not exist here.
- `scripts/goal2_sex_stratified/14_model_training_nested_cv.R:148` hard-codes
  `flat_train_CV_AUC = c(0.792, 0.966)` from a superseded run, and that value is
  written into `mr_nested_cv_summary.csv` as though computed.
- `mr_nested_cv_summary.csv` (0.816 F / 0.896 M) and `mr_final_panel_summary.csv`
  (0.809 F / 0.952 M) report different nested-CV AUCs from different scripts,
  with no reconciliation.
- `data/raw` is a symlink outside the project tree; not self-contained.
- No medication or disease-activity adjustment. With composition now handled,
  this is arguably the largest unaddressed biological confounder.
- No pre-registered analysis plan. The number of analytic branches is now large
  enough that a dated plan covering the remaining work would strengthen the
  multiplicity argument.
